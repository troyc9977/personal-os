# 0003: Contemplative floor — 10-minute morning meditation

## Status

Accepted — 2026-05-03

## Context

Contemplative practice has slipped. The last sustained period of real interior stillness was while meditating in Japan (months ago, possibly longer). There has been no formal practice this month. I have stated, in my own values, that contemplation is foundational and that acedia is the master temptation I most want to guard against — and yet the practice quietly went away while WardForge and DCC consumed mornings.

Reading is doing some of the interior work, but reading is not a substitute for sitting. Knowing this and doing nothing about it is its own form of dishonesty.

The pattern that killed the practice was clear: no anchor habit. No fixed time, no fixed length, no fixed place. "When I have time" became never.

## Decision

I am committing to a 10-minute morning meditation as the contemplative floor, with the following specifications:

- **Length**: 10 minutes. Small enough to actually happen, with room to grow once the floor holds.
- **Time**: immediately after morning coffee, before opening the laptop or any screens.
- **Place**: same chair every morning. Same seat = the practice happens.
- **Form**: silent sit, simple anchor (breath or the Jesus Prayer). Not guided, not app-based.
- **Frequency**: daily, including weekends. The practice does not get a day off.

If after two weeks the 10 minutes is holding, I extend by 5 minutes. If the practice is dying, the answer is not "more discipline" — it is to reduce further (5 min) or change the form (sit later in the day, walking practice, etc.). The floor matters more than the height.

No streak counting. No quantified-self overlay. The practice is its own measure.

## Consequences

### Positive
- A real anchor habit, which is the thing that was missing.
- 10 minutes is small enough that "I don't have time" is not an honest objection.
- Putting it before the laptop ensures it happens before WardForge consumes the morning.
- Re-establishes the daily rhythm of interior practice that everything else in the contemplative tradition builds on.

### Negative
- Risk of the practice becoming a checkbox rather than real practice. Mitigated by no streak counting and explicit watch-for-drift signals in `docs/projects/contemplative-practice.md`.
- Risk that 10 minutes becomes a ceiling rather than a floor. Mitigated by the 2-week extension trigger.
- Risk that the practice happens but doesn't deepen. The honest acceptance: 10 minutes of distracted sitting is still better than zero minutes, and the depth comes back with consistency. The first month is not for depth, it is for re-establishing the chair.

### Neutral
- This is not a contemplative revival. It is a floor. Real contemplative depth — sustained silence, the Jesus Prayer as continuous undercurrent, weekend retreats — is downstream of the floor holding for months.

## Alternatives considered

- **20-minute morning sit.** Rejected. Too aspirational. The reason the practice died was the gap between aspiration and reality.
- **Evening meditation.** Considered. Rejected because evenings are reading time and morning is what was lost. Anchoring to the lost slot is more important than anchoring to the convenient slot.
- **Multiple shorter sits throughout the day.** Considered. Rejected at this stage because variability kills habit formation. One fixed time, every day, until it holds.
- **An app-based practice.** Rejected. The contemplative tradition I am working in (Christian, hesychast, Augustine-adjacent) does not sit well in app form. Quiet sitting in a chair is the form.
- **No formal practice; trust reading.** Rejected. Reading is doing real interior work but is not the same thing. Pretending it is would be the kind of self-deception this ADR exists to guard against.

## Review

This ADR gets revisited at:
- **2 weeks (May 17)**: is the floor holding?
- **1 month (June 3)**: extend to 15 minutes if holding, reduce or restructure if not.
- **3 months (August 3)**: substantial review of the practice. Has depth returned? Is it still a checkbox? What needs to change?
