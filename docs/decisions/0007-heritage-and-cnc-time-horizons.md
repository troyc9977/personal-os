# 0007: Heritage and CNC acquisition theses — time horizons

## Status

Accepted — 2026-05-05

## Context

Two acquisition theses now live in the substrate.

`projects/heritage-restoration.md` describes acquiring a Toronto/Ontario heritage restoration business by 2028-2029 as the cylinder for the *beauty* lane of the 25-year project.

The May 2026 long-term goal list introduces "buy a CNC shop in 1-2 years" — a precision-machining business, a different industry from heritage restoration.

These are not the same goal. Different industries, different operator profiles, different valuations, different relationships to the cultural-output and beauty theses.

## Decision

- **CNC shop**: 1-2 year horizon. Active acquisition target. Type: precision manufacturing, AI-systems-improvement angle. Geography: Toronto / GTA. Becomes its own goal file at `goals/long-term/cnc-shop-acquisition.md`.
- **Heritage restoration**: 5+ year long-arc. Remains as the cylinder for the beauty lane. `projects/heritage-restoration.md` updated to reflect a 2030+ horizon (vs. the previous 2028-2029).
- Both theses stay. Neither replaces the other.

## Consequences

### Positive
- CNC operationalizes faster — a near-term acquisition target while WardForge is still maturing.
- Heritage stays alive without the pressure of a 2028 deadline that no longer fits the wider plan.
- Two parallel acquisition tracks make explicit a longstanding ambition.

### Negative
- Two acquisition theses is ambitious for one operator running WardForge in parallel. Risk of capital and attention fragmentation. Mitigated by clear sequencing: CNC first, heritage downstream.
- The CNC thesis is newer and less developed — operator network, valuation framework, and capital sources are all unbuilt.

### Neutral
- `projects/heritage-restoration.md` needs editing to reflect the new horizon.
- The CNC goal file's first milestone is "operator network mapping" — research before any concrete approach.

## Alternatives considered

- **Replace heritage with CNC**: rejected. The heritage thesis is real, just longer-arc.
- **CNC as a subgoal of heritage**: rejected. Different industries, distinct operations.
- **Drop CNC**: rejected. The AI-systems-improvement angle is genuinely interesting and matches existing builder strengths.
