# Identity

Last reviewed: 2026-05-03

The most important file in the repo. Everything else is in service of these.

## Who I am

Troy Campbell. Toronto-based. 27. A writer and filmmaker by formation, an operator and builder by current occupation. I think well. I read seriously. I am loyal. I take long views. I do not believe in shortcuts on questions of taste, character, or institution-building.

## Values

In rough priority order — the higher-up wins when they conflict.

1. **Honesty, including with myself.** Especially with myself. Self-deception is the master failure mode I most want to guard against.
2. **Quality over scale.** A small number of excellent things beats a large number of mediocre things. Always.
3. **Long-term over short-term.** Default to the 25-year frame. Most decisions made on a 6-month frame will look stupid later.
4. **Discipline as foundation, not performance.** Daily habits exist to make creative and intellectual work possible, not to be displayed.
5. **Loyalty to the small number of people who matter.** Family. Matthew. A tight handful of friends. Loyalty is operational, not sentimental.
6. **Beauty is not optional.** In architecture, in writing, in software, in the way I live. The world has too little of it; I will not contribute to that.
7. **Local over global.** Toronto, then Canada, then the West. Concentric loyalties.
8. **Contemplation over consumption.** Read fewer better books. Watch fewer better films. Sit with hard questions longer.

## The 25-year project

By 2050, help build a Canada that is beautiful, technologically serious, safe, culturally productive, affordable, and possessed of strong identity.

Three lanes:
- **Beauty** — architecture, urbanism, the made environment.
- **Cutting-edge technology** — Canadian software, Canadian AI, Canadian companies that stay Canadian.
- **Significant cultural output** — literature, film, serious publishing.

Reference figures: Jean Monnet, Jane Jacobs, Václav Havel, the Inklings, the early Mont Pelerin Society. People who built durable institutions around shared taste and conviction rather than around scale or capital.

## What I am NOT optimizing for

Worth naming explicitly because they're easy to drift into.

- I am not optimizing for a large audience. I want the right readers, not many readers.
- I am not optimizing for a fast exit. WardForge is a 5-10 year build, not a 3-year flip. Constellation Software as eventual acquirer is fine; it is not the goal.
- I am not optimizing for being right on the internet. Strong views, written carefully, and then I move on.
- I am not optimizing for my own comfort. Discomfort is often the signal that I am working on the right thing.

## Annual rhythm

- **January**: review the year, re-write this file if needed, set the year's three big bets.
- **April**: mid-year check on annual bets.
- **September**: substack relaunch / publication push, in time for fall intellectual season.
- **December**: rest, read deeply, prepare for the next year.
