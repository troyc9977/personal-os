# The 28th Floor

**Status**: drafted, revised heavily, currently dormant. Decide what's next.

Last updated: 2026-05-03

## What it is

Literary short story. Submitted to the Toronto Star Short Story Contest. Revised through nine versions with extensive editorial collaboration. Psychological fiction following a pattern-seeking protagonist through Toronto.

Themes: numerology, identity, meaning-making, the texture of a particular Toronto. DeLillo and Lynch are the most direct influences.

## What I want to do with it

Open question. Possibilities:

1. **Submit to literary magazines.** Real publication credit. Builds the writing track.
2. **Lightly polish and post on Substack** when the Substack launches. Anchor piece.
3. **Expand into a novella or novel.** The protagonist and the world have more in them than one short story holds.
4. **Leave it as it is.** It already did its work — the writing taught me what I needed to learn from it.

Decision pending. Worth a quarterly review.

## Why it matters

This was my first sustained piece of serious literary writing. The lessons from drafting and revising it apply to everything I'll write. The value is partly the artifact, mostly what the artifact taught me about my own writing.

## Notes for future me

- The story is good. Trust that. Don't keep revising it forever.
- The opening was always strong; the middle improved through revision; the ending found its real shape only at version 7 or 8.
- Pattern-seeking as a theme is a hidden door into a larger novel-shape if I want it.
