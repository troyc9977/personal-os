# Family and Friends

**Status**: active priority. Second only to WardForge.

Last reviewed: 2026-05-03

## What this is

The relationships that matter most. Not a CRM. Not a list of contacts to "manage." A reminder that the people I am loyal to come second only to the work I am building, and that the substrate should reflect that.

The detailed relational context lives in `docs/people.md`. This file is the operational frame: what I am trying to do for the people I love, and how I make sure I actually do it.

## Why this is in priority position 2

Family and friends sit just below WardForge in the priority order — above reading, health, contemplation, writing, and DCC. The reasons:

- Loyalty to the small number of people who matter is one of my stated values, and a value that doesn't shape behavior is not a real value.
- The 25-year project is meaningless if I arrive at 2050 having built institutions while the people I love drifted away.
- WardForge intensity creates exactly the conditions in which relationships erode quietly. The priority position is a deliberate counterweight.
- The recent change in my partnership has made clear that I do not want to be a person who lets relationships fall behind work without noticing.

## What this looks like in practice

Not a daily ask. The discipline is monthly and quarterly:

- **Monthly**: update `people.md`. Notice who I haven't spoken to in too long. Reach out to one person on the "drifted from but don't want to lose" list.
- **Quarterly**: review the inner circle and friends sections. Ask whether I have actually been the brother/friend/collaborator I want to be. Honest answers, even when uncomfortable.
- **Anytime**: when something meaningful happens in someone's life, write it down. When I owe a follow-up, log it. When I think of someone, the question is whether to message them now or note the impulse for later.

## Specific commitments

Not aspirational, just floor-level:

- **Family**: meaningful contact at least monthly, more often when possible.
- **Matthew**: weekly sync via the WardForge cadence, plus the relational layer above the operational one. He is a co-founder and a friend; both layers need attention.
- **Close friends**: each one gets meaningful contact at least quarterly. "Meaningful" = a conversation, a meal, a real exchange, not a like on a post.
- **The drifted list**: one outreach per month minimum.

## What this is NOT

- A networking project.
- An optimization exercise.
- Performance of being a good friend.

It is the recognition that the people I love deserve a place in the substrate, and that without naming it explicitly, the work I am building will quietly absorb the time and attention they need.

## Watch-for signals of drift

- I have not initiated a personal conversation with anyone outside Matthew or family in over two weeks.
- Someone tells me about something important in their life and I realize I had no idea it was happening.
- I find myself rationalizing why "right now" is not the time to reach out to someone I care about.
- Calendar contains zero non-work non-family social time for the next 4 weeks.

When these signals appear, the response is to clear an evening that week and reach out to the person I most need to see.
