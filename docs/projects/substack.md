# Substack / Writing Practice

**Status**: in development. Public launch September-October 2026, contingent on preconditions below.

Last updated: 2026-05-03

## What it is

Personal Substack. Biweekly cadence. The intellectual through-line for the public-facing work — combining DCC practitioner experience, WardForge build context, and broader reading on Canadian institutions, infrastructure, and modernization.

## Positioning

Building toward being Canada's foremost voice on infrastructure delivery and political modernization — the practitioner-intellectual lane that almost nobody currently occupies in Canada. Most infrastructure writing is academic, opaque, or partisan. The opportunity is direct, technically literate, civically serious writing for an educated general audience.

*Comment Magazine* is the first target outside-publication.

## Preconditions for public launch

Locking these in so the launch date doesn't quietly drift.

- **~300 X followers** before public launch. Real audience, real engagement, not just a number — but the number is the rough threshold.
- **3-5 backlog pieces drafted** so the first weeks have meaningful content and aren't a writer's-block performance.
- **At least one piece on the decline of the West essay** in some form (the long-circling one).

If these are not met by September 2026, the launch shifts. Not the other way around.

## The piece I keep circling

An analysis of the decline of the West.

This is the piece that has been quiet for too long. The discipline now is not "wait until the thesis is sharp enough" — it is to open a document, write the worst version of the first paragraph, and iterate from there. Starting badly is the practice; starting well is the eventual outcome.

If this piece does not exist in any form by end of June 2026, I should ask why and either commit harder or release the idea.

## Reading anchor

- Bent Flyvbjerg, *How Big Things Get Done* — the master text on infrastructure delivery.
- Infrastructure Ontario frameworks.
- Auditor General reports (Ontario and federal).
- Caro on Robert Moses (different era, same animal).
- For the decline-of-the-West piece: a wider net — Spengler, Burnham, MacIntyre, contemporary Canadian / British / American conservative thought. Build the reading list as the piece develops.

- Productivity decline and capital misallocation in Canadian housing/real estate.
- The 52 GTA infrastructure authorities by domain and jurisdiction — *(research from earlier work)*.
- Political modernization without losing what's worth keeping.
- The contemplative roots of serious public work.
- The decline-of-the-West essay as a longer arc threading through several pieces.

## Cadence

- **Now → September 2026**: build X following toward 300, draft backlog, sit down and start the decline-of-the-West piece.
- **September-October 2026**: public launch with backlog ready.
- **Post-launch**: biweekly posts, 1500-3000 words, one publication-target essay every quarter.

## What I am NOT writing

- Daily political commentary.
- Hot takes on the news cycle.
- Anything that requires being chronically online.

## Discipline

- Writing time: Saturday mornings primarily, Sunday afternoons as needed.
- Drafts in Obsidian / a separate writing repo, finished posts published on Substack.
- A piece is finished when it's true and well-said, not when it's long enough.
- Tweet 2x/day on writing days. The X following is built by being interesting, not by being constantly on.
