# Filmmaking

**Status**: paused until WardForge Phase 1 ships (October 2026). Resumes Q1 2028.

Last updated: 2026-05-03

## What it is

A serious filmmaking practice, building toward a feature film by year two of the production company being set up. Reference: Kubrick's preproduction methodology, adapted into a 19-week system I've sketched out previously.

## Why it's part of the long-arc

Cultural output cylinder of the 25-year project. The cylinder I am most patient about. Films take longer than software. The work I do here will not bear public fruit for years; that is correct.

## Plan

### Year 1 (post-launch, ~2027)
- Set up the production company structure.
- Apply to relevant Telefilm / Ontario Creates / TIFF Industry programs.
- Make four short films. Each is a calibration exercise — different genre, different approach, different lesson.
- Document everything. The point of year one is learning, not making a masterpiece.

### Year 2 (~2028)
- Feature film preproduction using the 19-week Kubrick-style system.
- Production.
- Post.

### Beyond
- Stays a quiet, serious cylinder. Not a content business.
