## Reading principles

- Re-reading > new reading on the canonical works.
- Stop when a book isn't working. Life is short.
- Notes for serious books, no notes for entertainment.
- One serious book + one lighter book in parallel is a good rhythm.
- Don't review books for an audience. Read for self-formation.
