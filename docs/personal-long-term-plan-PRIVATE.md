# Personal long-term plan

## Troy Goodfellow — private working document

Status: Draft, April 2026. Personal notes, not company strategy.
Revisit quarterly; revise as circumstances change.

## Identity decisions (settled as of April 2026)

**Not running for office.** I will not pursue elected office personally.
My role is operator and infrastructure builder, not candidate.

**WardForge stays nonpartisan publicly.** As CEO of WardForge, I do not
take public partisan positions. The company serves any campaign willing
to pay regardless of ideology.

**Personal politics separate from business.** I have personal political
views and may engage with political work privately, but my role at
WardForge does not reflect or advance these views publicly.

**Behind-the-scenes only for partisan work.** Any partisan involvement
happens privately — advising, contributing, supporting — without public
identification as a partisan actor. Recognize that behind-the-scenes work
may eventually become public; plan for that contingency.

## Time horizon

I'm building a 6-10 year company. WardForge is the primary work through
2032 minimum. Personal political infrastructure work is secondary and
deferred to when WardForge is established.

The compounding pattern: WardForge success generates resources,
relationships, and credibility that make later political infrastructure
work plausible. The reverse path — pursuing political work prematurely —
produces neither business success nor meaningful political impact.

## Phase A: Building (2026-2028)

**Primary focus: WardForge.** Win Toronto/Hamilton municipal 2026,
expand to Ontario provincial 2027-2028, federal 2028 if cycle aligns.

**Networking:** Attend political events, build relationships across
the political spectrum. Don't actively cultivate partisan identity.
Be known as a software founder who happens to be politically informed,
not as a political actor.

**Reading and learning:** Continue substantive engagement with political
theory, campaign management literature, electoral history. This is
foundational for product work and personal preparation.

**Avoid:** Public partisan statements, donations to politically partisan
PACs that become public record (donate quietly via personal channels if
desired), speaking at explicitly partisan conferences as a featured
speaker.

**Acceptable:** Private advice to candidates or organizations across
the spectrum, attendance at events without speaking roles, contributions
to non-partisan civic organizations.

## Phase B: Established (2028-2030)

**Business state:** WardForge operational across Canadian municipal,
provincial, federal tiers. UK expansion underway. Possibly first hires
of customer success and engineering staff.

**Personal positioning:** Established as a successful political
infrastructure founder. Comfortable speaking publicly about software
and AI in politics without taking partisan positions.

**Decision point: ecosystem work.** By 2029, decide whether to begin
building connective infrastructure for aligned organizations.
Considerations:

- Has WardForge reached enough scale to fund this work without
  distracting from operations?
- Is there a credible coalition of organizations willing to work with
  shared infrastructure?
- What's my role — funder, organizer, operator?
- How does this preserve WardForge's nonpartisan positioning?

**Decision point: slate involvement.** Same timeframe, similar
considerations. If pursuing, structure as separate entity from WardForge
with clean operational and financial separation.

## Phase C: Influence (2030-2035)

**Business state:** WardForge as established political infrastructure
across multiple Anglo-democracies. Significant team, possibly multi-
million ARR, established competitive position.

**Personal possibilities (not commitments):**

- Founder of a connective infrastructure organization linking aligned
  Canadian nonprofits, advocacy groups, media organizations during
  election cycles
- Funder of aligned organizations (think tanks, advocacy groups,
  media properties) using WardForge revenue
- Advisor to political parties on AI and infrastructure (through
  formal or informal channels)
- Architect of a slate or movement infrastructure (separate from
  WardForge)

These are options, not commitments. Decisions deferred until WardForge
position justifies the resource allocation.

## Strategic principles for political work

**Never use WardForge to advance personal political positioning.**
The company's tools serve customers, not the founder's politics.

**Maintain founder/business separation rigorously.** Even private
political work shouldn't bleed into company decisions about which
customers to serve or how the product works.

**Plan for eventual public scrutiny.** Behind-the-scenes work eventually
becomes public when one becomes a notable figure. Make decisions you'd
be willing to defend publicly even if currently private.

**Resources before influence.** Generate resources through business
success first. Apply resources to political work later. Don't sacrifice
the business for premature political ambition.

**Compounding over speed.** Political infrastructure takes decades to
build credibility. Don't rush. The right work in 2030 is better than
fast work in 2027.

## Personal vulnerabilities to watch

Things about myself I should monitor:

**Distraction risk.** I'm intellectually engaged by political ideas and
strategic conversations. The work that builds the company is often
boring — customer conversations, code review, operational discipline.
Watch for the temptation to substitute strategy thinking for execution.

**Conflict aversion in customer outreach.** Multiple weeks of deferred
customer outreach despite stated intentions. The discomfort of cold
outreach is real but cannot be allowed to substitute with comfortable
engineering work indefinitely.

**Identity attachment to product.** WardForge is meaningful to me. The
risk: making product decisions based on what's intellectually exciting
rather than what customers will pay for. Listen to customer signal even
when it's less interesting than my own ideas.

**Partisan temptation as the company succeeds.** Success creates platform
that's tempting to use for political voice. The discipline of staying
quietly partisan rather than publicly partisan will get harder as
WardForge grows. Plan for this temptation; resist it.

## Practices to maintain

**Quarterly review of this document.** Update based on what's actually
happening. Don't let this become aspirational fiction; keep it grounded
in current reality.

**Annual review of trajectory.** Am I doing what this document says?
If not, why not? Update the document or change the behavior.

**Honest assessment of energy and motivation.** Six years is long.
Watch for signs of burnout, shifting motivation, or evolving priorities.
Adjust the plan to match the human, not vice versa.

**Maintain non-WardForge life.** Reading, writing, filmmaking,
relationships. These are not luxuries; they're the foundation of being
able to sustain meaningful work over years.

## Closing principle

The most important thing I can do for both WardForge and any future
political work is to be successful at the current work. Distraction by
the next phase undermines the current phase. Trust the sequence: win
each phase before pursuing the next.
