# Latin — Lingua Latina Per Se Illustrata, Pars I

## Why this matters

Latin opens primary sources across two thousand years of Western intellectual
history. Cicero, Livy, Augustine, Aquinas, the Vulgate — currently mediated
through translators. Reading them in the original is its own kind of access.
Lower priority right now but worth being captured rather than forgotten.

## Current state

Small prior beginner experience. *Lingua Latina Per Se Illustrata: Pars I:
Familia Romana* (Hans Ørberg) on hand. Natural-method approach — Latin taught
via Latin from chapter one, no English grammar drills.

## Approach (when active)

One capitulum per week, read aloud, work the Pensa exercises, re-read prior
chapters periodically to consolidate. No translation crutch.

## Activation trigger

After RidingPulse v1 launches (Aug 1, 2026) and the biweekly Substack cadence
is established. Earliest realistic activation September 2026.

## Open
